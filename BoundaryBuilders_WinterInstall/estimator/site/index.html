Your privacy

Boundary Builders (Surrey, BC) uses this page to give you an approx price and to follow up with you about your project.

What we collect

  • Your name, phone number and email.
  • Your answers: what you want built, the materials you picked, your measurements and the prices shown to you.
  • Photos you choose to add. If you ask for a site visit, your address and the times that suit you.
  • Basic technical details we need to run this page and block spam, like the time you sent your request.

Why we collect it

To prepare your estimate, contact you about it, book your site visit and send you a written quote. We don’t sell your information, and we don’t add you to marketing lists.

Where it’s kept and for how long

Your request is stored on our website’s server and sent to our business email. Some of the services we use may store data outside Canada. We keep quote requests for up to 2 years and then delete them, unless you hire us, in which case we keep the job records we need for warranty and tax purposes.

This page also uses Meta (Facebook) and/or Google measurement tools so we can see which of our ads lead to quote requests. They don’t receive your name, phone, email or answers from this page.

On this device

Your answers are saved in this browser so you can pick up where you left off. “Start a new quote” clears them.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time. Email info@boundarybuilders.ca or call (778) 885-1042.
