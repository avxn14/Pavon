## 02_wall_drainage: Why retaining walls fail: water

- Post date: 2026-10-13 10:00
- Series pill: WALL TIP #1 | Category: RETAINING WALLS
- Cover title: WALL / *DRAINAGE* (photo A)
- File: final/02_wall_drainage.mp4 | Cover: final/02_wall_drainage_cover.jpg

Caption:

The number one reason retaining walls fail in the Lower Mainland is water. A well-built block wall typically has about 12 inches of drain rock behind it and a perforated pipe at the base that drains to an approved outlet. Bulging, leaning or weeping? Have an engineer take a look.

## 03_fence_posts: This is how a fence should be installed: the posts

- Post date: 2026-10-15 10:00
- Series pill: FENCE TIP #2 | Category: FENCE
- Cover title: FENCE POSTS / *DONE RIGHT* (photo C)
- File: final/03_fence_posts.mp4 | Cover: final/03_fence_posts_cover.jpg

Caption:

How a fence should be installed in Metro Vancouver, starting with the posts: request a free BC 1 Call locate before you dig, set about a third of the post in the ground (2-3 ft for a 6 ft fence), put gravel underneath, crown the concrete so rain runs off, and space posts 6-8 ft apart. Rules of thumb; every site is different.

#boundarybuilders #fenceinstallation #fencebuilder #cedarfence #metrovancouver

Why pavers sink in the Lower Mainland, and the base that stops it. The industry minimum for a patio is 4 inches of compacted base on ideal ground; on our wet clay plan at least 6, often more, compacted in layers over fabric, and more again for driveways.

## 05_turf_base: Artificial turf puddling? Check the base

- Post date: 2026-10-19 10:00
- Series pill: YARD TIP #1 | Category: LANDSCAPING
- Cover title: TURF? IT'S / ALL *BASE* (photo A)
- File: final/05_turf_base.mp4 | Cover: final/05_turf_base_cover.jpg

Caption:

Puddles on artificial turf in the Lower Mainland usually point to the base. A typical install uses a weed barrier and 3-4 inches of compacted crushed gravel (wet clay may need more, or a drain), sloped away from the house, and never plastic underneath. In leaf season, blow the leaves off before they hold moisture and feed moss.
