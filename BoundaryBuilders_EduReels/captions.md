## 01_leaning_fence: Leaning fence? The 3 usual causes

Caption:

Leaning fence after a Lower Mainland windstorm? It's usually rot at ground level, posts set too shallow, or soggy soil plus wind on a solid fence. After the first big storm, push on your posts and check for wobble at the base.
