## 10_cedar_care: Cedar resists rot, but it's not rot-proof

- Post date: 2026-10-29 10:00
- Series pill: FENCE TIP #4 | Category: FENCE
- Cover title: MAKE CEDAR / *LAST* (photo A)
- File: final/10_cedar_care.mp4 | Cover: final/10_cedar_care_cover.jpg

Caption:

Cedar fences in the Lower Mainland resist rot, but cedar isn't rot-proof. Keep the boards off the soil with a kickboard, cap the posts (cut ends soak up water about 11x faster), use stainless or hot-dip galvanized fasteners, and stain new cedar within a couple of weeks once it's dry, on a dry day that meets the stain label's temperature.
